Jatt Di Queen Set for Valentine’s Day 2027 Release; Punjabi Movie to Hit Cinemas on February 12

Valentine’s Day 2027 is set to receive a Punjabi cinema offering with the upcoming film Jatt Di Queen. The movie, presented by Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions, is scheduled to arrive in cinemas on 12 February 2027, just before Valentine’s Day.

The announcement has generated excitement among Punjabi movie audiences, especially after the makers unveiled the film's first-look poster.

Jatt Di Queen First Look Receives Audience Support

The first-look poster of Jatt Di Queen was unveiled by the makers recently and received an encouraging response online. Fans and followers showed their support through likes, comments and shares.

Following the response, the team behind the film thanked audiences for their continued support and described the poster reveal as the beginning of the film's journey.

A New Punjabi Movie for Valentine's Day

With Jatt Di Queen releasing on 12 February 2027, the film will reach cinemas around the Valentine's Day weekend.

The release date gives Punjabi cinema audiences another option for their Valentine's Day plans, while the makers are inviting audiences to celebrate the occasion in cinemas.

The film is produced by Prabh Goraya and Vicky Kamboj and directed by Arun Kumar Barnwal. Sumit Bhatt has written the film.

Who Stars in Jatt Di Queen?

The upcoming Punjabi movie features King B Chouhan, Rumman Ahmad, Malwai Partap, Sammy Gill, Honey Mattu, Neha Dayal, Pahulpreet Singh, Lucky Kalsi, Deush Mehra and Sukhdev Barnala.

The wide ensemble gives Jatt Di Queen a strong cast lineup as the film prepares for its theatrical journey.

Music by Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy

The music department features Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy as Music Directors.

The film's lyrics are credited to Happy Raikoti, Youngveer, Amber Vashisht, Abeer and Sufraaz. Malwai Partap is credited as a Playback Singer, while Manoj Gadarpuriya and Sachin Dahar are among the singers associated with the movie.

Behind the Camera

The cinematography team includes Harnek Singh and Rah Sharma, while Rohit Dhiman serves as Editor. Ajay Maurya is the Executive Producer.

The film also features Gurjeet Singh as Title Song Editor and Monika Rani as Title Song Choreographer. Deush Mehra is credited as Choreographer.

Post-production is being handled by Rabab Studio, with AME Digital as Digital Partner.

When Will Jatt Di Queen Release?

The makers have confirmed that Jatt Di Queen will release in cinemas on 12 February 2027.

With its first-look poster now out and the Valentine's Day release date locked, the film is expected to remain on the radar of Punjabi cinema fans as more promotional material is unveiled.
